Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 6, 2026

Tab Setu is a browser extension designed to help users manage and organize their browser tabs efficiently.

Information We Access

Tab Setu may access browser tab information such as tab titles, URLs, and window details only to provide its tab management features. This access is limited to the current session and is necessary for the core functionality of the extension.

Information We Collect

Tab Setu does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal information to any external server. We believe your data belongs to you.

Local Storage

Any data saved by Tab Setu is stored locally in the user’s browser using browser storage APIs. This data remains on the user’s device unless the user removes the extension data or clears browser storage manually.

Third-Party Sharing

Tab Setu does not share user data with third parties. No data is ever sent to our servers or any third-party services.

Analytics and Advertising

Tab Setu does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services. We do not track your behavior or serve ads.

Data Deletion

Users can delete stored data at any time by clearing the extension’s storage, resetting the extension, or uninstalling Tab Setu from the browser.
